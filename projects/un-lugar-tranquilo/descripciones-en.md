## Video format: "Music → Drawing → Story" (25–30 min)

Repeat the same structure in every video; change the scene and the story.
Chapters need the first stamp at 0:00 and at least 3 stamps, each 10+ seconds apart.

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome piano | Slow watercolor scene, soft piano + strings |
| 8:00 | Drawing time | Child's hands (or an animated illustration) draw the scene, slow pace, music continues softly |
| 13:00 | Story time | Short narrated story, very quiet piano underneath, volume drops during dialogue |
| 19:00 | Piano & nature | Back to music, same scene, animals drifting through |
| 24:00 | Drawing finishes | The drawing is completed and colored |
| 28:00 | Goodnight | Quiet ending, piano fades out |

Production notes:
- Real children on camera under Made for Kids: get written parental consent, show only hands/drawing, no face, no name. Alternative: an illustrated drawing animation (safer, consistent with the watercolor identity).
- The story must be original and the voice calm; narration over low piano only.
- Keep each video's drawing + story different so videos are not near-identical (monetization risk).

# Institutional use: schools, daycares, learning centers

## What changes
1. **Predictability is the product.** Same order, same length, same voice, same music style in every video. Kids (especially those who rely on routine) know what comes next.
2. **Modular library, not only long videos.** Each segment is also published alone so staff can pick what fits the moment.
3. **Visual cues on screen.** A small, quiet progress bar or "first / then" strip (Music → Draw → Story) at the corner, plus a slow countdown for transitions.
4. **Teacher / caregiver support.** Each video gets a printable one-page guide (free download, link in description).
5. **Not therapy, not a treatment.** It is calm music, drawing and stories with values. Never say it treats, cures or helps a condition. Say "calm", "gentle", "predictable".

## Draw-along design (fine motor friendly)
- Start with the shape names ("a circle for the lake"), draw each element slowly, pause 3 seconds between steps.
- Use only simple shapes: circle, line, curve, triangle.
- Show a finished picture first, then the steps ("first-then" logic).
- Hands only or animated pencil: no faces on camera.

## Story design
- 5–8 min, one simple idea, repeated phrases, slow narration.
- Core themes (one per story, rotating): **sharing, waiting your turn, respect, helping others**. Occasional: saying goodnight, trying again.
- Pause card at the end: "What did the little duck feel?" (teacher can pause).
- Narrator: one consistent calm voice (fixed for the whole channel).

## Rights and permissions (important for institutions)
- All music original or with verified commercial license; keep the license proofs in a folder per video.
- Write in the description: "Free to play in classrooms, daycares and centers. Please do not re-upload or sell." (check with your own legal review).
- Made for Kids on every video; comments will be off. Consider a separate email / website form for educators' requests.
