# Story 1 — The Little Duck and the Quiet Morning

**Value:** waiting your turn
**Length:** about 6 minutes at a slow, calm pace (~650 words)
**Placement:** Video 1, "A Morning by the Lake," at 13:00
**Music:** very soft piano underneath; volume drops further during dialogue
**Voice:** one calm, warm narrator. Pause 2–3 seconds where marked (…).

---

**[Scene: a quiet lake at sunrise, soft mist, lily pads]**

Early in the morning, the lake was very quiet.

The water was smooth like a mirror. The sun was just waking up behind the trees.

On the grassy bank lived a mother duck and her three little ducklings.

The smallest one was called Pip.

…

Pip loved the lake. He loved the water, the lily pads, and the little ripples that danced on the surface.

"Mama," said Pip softly, "can we swim now?"

Mama Duck smiled.

"Soon, Pip. First, we must wait for the mist to lift. Then the lake will be ready for us."

…

Pip sat down on the soft grass.

He wanted to swim right away. His little feet wiggled. His wings fluttered.

But he took a slow, deep breath.

In… and out.

…

**[Scene: a heron stands very still at the edge of the water]**

Beside the lake stood a tall, gray heron.

The heron was fishing. She stood very, very still.

"Good morning," whispered Pip.

"Good morning, little one," the heron whispered back. "Shhh. I am waiting for my breakfast."

"How long will you wait?" asked Pip.

"As long as it takes," said the heron. "Waiting is part of the morning."

…

Pip watched. The mist drifted slowly across the water. A leaf floated by. A dragonfly rested on a reed.

Pip noticed something. When he waited quietly, he could see so many beautiful things.

…

**[Scene: the mist lifts; a family of turtles on a log]**

Soon, the mist began to rise.

A little turtle was climbing onto a log in the sunshine. Then another turtle. And another.

"Excuse me," said the first turtle. "May I go first? I am slow, and I need a sunny spot."

"Of course," said Pip. "You go first. I will wait."

The turtle smiled.

"Thank you, little duck."

…

One by one, the turtles climbed up. Pip waited for each of them.

And when they were all settled in the warm sun, the turtles said together:

"Thank you for waiting, Pip."

Pip felt warm inside, even warmer than the sun.

…

**[Scene: the lake is clear, golden light on the water]**

At last, the mist was gone.

The lake sparkled in the morning light.

"Now, Pip," said Mama Duck. "It is our turn."

Pip's heart gave a happy little hop.

Mama Duck went first, gliding slowly into the water. Then the big sister duckling. Then the brother.

And then…

"Now you, Pip."

Pip stepped into the lake.

The water was cool and soft.

He paddled slowly. He made little ripples. He floated past the lily pads, past the heron, past the turtles on their log.

…

"The lake was worth waiting for," said Pip.

"It always is," said Mama Duck.

**[Scene: the ducklings swimming in a line, gentle sunlight]**

And so the little ducklings swam together, slowly and quietly, on the calm blue lake.

The sun climbed higher. The birds began to sing.

And Pip learned something very special that morning:

When we wait, we make space for others.

When we wait, we notice the beautiful world around us.

And when it is our turn, it feels even sweeter.

…

*The end.*

---

## Pause card (on screen after the story, 10 seconds, no narration)

**Think and talk (for teachers and caregivers)**
1. What did Pip do while he waited?
2. How did the turtles feel when Pip let them go first?
3. What can you do while you wait for your turn?

## Illustration list (for the animator)
1. Lake at sunrise, mist, lily pads (no hard outlines, watercolor).
2. Mother duck and three ducklings on the grassy bank; Pip is the smallest.
3. Heron standing still at the water's edge.
4. Mist rising; leaf and dragonfly.
5. Turtles climbing onto a sunny log.
6. Ducklings swimming in a line over golden water.

Palette: only the 6 official colors (Sage, Luna, Cielo, Tierra, Papel, Tinta).
Keep movements slow: mist drifting, ripples, a leaf floating.
