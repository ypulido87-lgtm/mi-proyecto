# Trusted Research Sources

Use the most relevant primary authority for each material claim and verify the exact page is current for the relevant year.

Federal tax:
- Internal Revenue Service — https://www.irs.gov/
- U.S. Department of the Treasury — https://home.treasury.gov/
- Congress.gov — https://www.congress.gov/
- Federal Register — https://www.federalregister.gov/

Payroll/employment:
- U.S. Department of Labor — https://www.dol.gov/
- Social Security Administration — https://www.ssa.gov/
- IRS employment tax guidance — https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employment-taxes

Florida:
- Florida Department of Revenue — https://floridarevenue.com/
- Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) — https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/
- Florida Legislature — https://www.leg.state.fl.us/

Research hierarchy: statute/regulation or authoritative government guidance; relevant agency publication; reputable professional analysis; reputable journalism for context.

Never treat social media, influencer videos, promotional tax websites, unverified blogs, or AI-generated summaries as primary authority.

Record the specific source used, not merely an agency homepage when a precise page exists. Never fabricate a URL.
