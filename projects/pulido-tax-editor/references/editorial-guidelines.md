# Editorial Guidelines — Pulido Tax & Accounting LLC

Editorial promise: every article should help the reader say, “Ahora entiendo algo que antes me parecía complicado.” Education and trust come before sales.

Voice: professional, approachable, practical, calm, and clear. Spanish should sound natural to a broad U.S. Spanish-speaking audience. English should sound natural to U.S. readers. Do not create literal translations.

Default length: 600–900 words. Shorter is welcome when sufficient. Longer should be exceptional.

Use short paragraphs, descriptive headings, explained terminology, and examples only when they improve understanding. Separate general rules from exceptions and state uncertainty explicitly.

Categories: Taxes; Tax Credits; Accounting & Bookkeeping; Payroll; Small Business.

Evergreen fallback ideas: estimated taxes for self-employed workers; W-2 vs. 1099; single-member LLC basics; separating personal/business expenses; records to keep; bank reconciliations; preparing books for tax season; payroll mistakes; worker classification; Schedule C; business deductions; tax-credit education.

CTA: keep it short and relevant. Never promise outcomes.

Prohibited: clickbait, manufactured urgency, “secret loopholes,” guaranteed refunds/savings, proposals presented as law, copied competitor content, SEO padding, or individualized eligibility conclusions without sufficient facts.
